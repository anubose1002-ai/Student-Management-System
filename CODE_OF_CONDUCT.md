# Code of Conduct

We are committed to maintaining a respectful, welcoming, and inclusive community.

### Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Respect different opinions and backgrounds.
- Do not harass, discriminate, or personally attack others.
- Do not share private information without permission.

### Reporting

If you experience or witness inappropriate behavior, please contact the project maintainer privately:

**Email:** email@example.com  
**GitHub:** @anubose1002-ai

All reports will be handled respectfully and as confidentially as reasonably possible.

Unacceptable behavior may result in removal from the project or community.
